Paninaro magazine, August 1987: the paninari of Ancona and Bari.

i paninari / named after the panino.

The paninari's own monthly ran city reports. These pages list Ancona's hangouts and shops, rank the labels ("troppo giusto", "regolare", "troppo scarso"), then cross to Bari.

Paninaro no. 20, August 1987.
