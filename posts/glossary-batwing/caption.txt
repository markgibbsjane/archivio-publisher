Knitwear glossary, part 4: the batwing sleeve.

manica a pipistrello / batwing sleeve.

Cut in one piece with the body, with an armhole that drops almost to the waist: the old dolman sleeve, back as the batwing in the 1980s.

anny blatt no. 59, mid-'80s; bettina no. 22 (LANG).
