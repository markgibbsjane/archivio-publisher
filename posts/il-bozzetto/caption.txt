Late-1980s Italian fashion, drawn by hand.

il bozzetto / the sketch.

"Gli stilisti interpretano le tendenze dell'industria," the magazine called the section: designers interpret the industry's trends. Seven of the eight pages credit a designer "per" a company.

1. Enrico Coveri per Filpuma
2. Alessandra Bagni per Effe Uno
3. Ornella Bignami per Lane Folco
4. Lucio Antonucci per Natural Cloth Filati
5. Vincenzo Mosca per Alessandra Filati
6. Paola Rossi per Bertrand
7. Not credited on the page
8. Alberto Lattuada per Avia

Design & Italian Fashion, no. 24, Donna 1988/89.
