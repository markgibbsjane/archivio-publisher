Late-1980s Italian knitwear, drawn by hand.

il bozzetto / the sketch.

"Gli stilisti interpretano le tendenze dell'industria," the magazine called the section: designers interpret the industry's trends. Each drawing is signed by a designer "per" a company, mostly yarn mills.

1. Alberto Lattuada per Avia
2. Enrico Coveri per Filpuma
3. Vincenzo Mosca per Alessandra Filati
4. Lucio Antonucci per Natural Cloth Filati
5. Paola Rossi per Bertrand
6. Alessandra Bagni per Effe Uno
7. Ornella Bignami per Lane Folco
8. Not credited on the page

Design & Italian Fashion, no. 24, Donna 1988/89.
