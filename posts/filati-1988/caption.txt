1988/89 Italian yarns, and the knits made from them.

il filato / the yarn.

At Pitti Filati, the Florence yarn fair founded in 1977, young designers were called in to interpret each mill's yarn on the runway. The magazine printed the swatches too, mill by mill.

1. Filati Be.Mi.Va., designed by Lorena Albisani
2. Be.Mi.Va., Igea Oro, Ilaria, Olivo Filati, Natural Cloth Filati, Ledal
3. Texwell, designed by Michel Hardy
4. Natural Cloth Filati, Sildorex Givitex, Texwell, Quadrifoglio, Igea Oro, Ecafil
5. A machine knit made to look handmade
6. Eurofil, Filatura di Grignasco, Uno Manifattura Filati, Bertrand, Quadrifoglio
7. Pure cashmere in natural white, punched with needle spaces

Design & Italian Fashion, no. 24, Donna 1988/89.
