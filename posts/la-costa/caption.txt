1988/89 Italian knitwear in rib.

la costa / rib. Coste, plural.

Knit and purl stitches sit in columns side by side, so the fabric stretches across and springs back. That's why it goes at cuffs, hems and necklines.

From the Pitti Filati show pages. Design & Italian Fashion, no. 24, Donna 1988/89.
